Privacy Policy

GX 4k · Last updated: October 22, 2026

GX 4k (“the App”) is a personal desktop application for Windows, created by AlfredGx12, that optimizes the user’s own video clips and lets the user send them to their own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Information the App accesses

  • Your video files. Files you choose are processed locally on your computer. They are not uploaded anywhere except to the platform(s) you explicitly select.
  • TikTok (Login Kit / Content Posting API). When you connect TikTok, the App receives an access token and your public display name (scope user.info.basic). With scope video.upload, the App sends the video you selected to your own TikTok drafts. The App does not read your videos, followers, messages or any other account data.
  • YouTube and Instagram. If you connect them, the App receives only the tokens needed to upload the videos you select to your own channel or account.

2. Where data is stored

Settings and access tokens are stored only on your own computer, in the folder .gx4k inside your user profile. The developer does not operate any server, does not receive your data, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta/Instagram, directly from your computer, when you choose to publish. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

4. Your control

You can disconnect any account inside the App at any time, which deletes its token from your computer. You can also revoke the App’s access from the permissions settings of your TikTok, Google or Instagram account. Uninstalling the App lets you delete the local settings folder.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
